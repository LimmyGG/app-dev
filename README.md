# Aladdin

> *"A whole new world..."*

**Aladdin** is a Disney story about a street-smart guy in Agrabah who finds a magic lamp, frees a Genie, and tries to win the heart of Princess Jasmine.

## Quick Info

- **Type:** Animated (1992) & Live-Action (2019)
- **Setting:** Agrabah
- **Famous Songs:** *A Whole New World*, *Friend Like Me*, *Prince Ali*

## Main Characters

- **Aladdin:** The "diamond in the rough" street rat.
- **Jasmine:** The independent princess.
- **Genie:** The funny, powerful cosmic entity.
- **Jafar:** The evil royal advisor.
